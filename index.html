    Phil is a Dubai Guitarist Singer#

    Ever since 2010 when he first moved to Dubai, the New Zealand born Phil Doughty has become the most sought after accomplished professional performing Dubai guitarist singer in the United Arab Emirates.

    Phil’s influences & style#

    Phil is described as a virtuoso guitarist, with his styles encompassing Rock, Blues, Jazz, Fusion, and Funky-Soul. He draws inspiration from renowned blues-rock guitarists such as Joe Satriani, Stevie Ray Vaughan, Andy Timmons, Eric Johnson, Eric Clapton, Jimmy Page, and Jeff Beck. Despite his influences, Phil has developed his own easily recognizable style. Rock, Blues, and Funky dance music are among his strongest genres as a guitarist. Phil is renowned as a versatile guitarist, capable of performing well in various styles. With over seventeen years of full-time solo, duo, and up to ten-piece live band performance experience, this Dubai-based guitarist has captivated audiences worldwide and has earned acclaim from high-profile corporate clients and companies.

    Just another Singing Dubai Guitarist?#

    The United Arab Emirates hosts a diverse community of musicians, including Dubai guitarists hailing from across the globe. Among them, some also showcase their vocal talents, though few reach the pinnacle of greatness. Phil stands out as a shining example in this elite category. Known for his meticulous attention to detail and unwavering pursuit of perfection, Phil approaches every endeavor with fervor.

    Whether crafting a new musical composition, honing his skills as a Dubai guitarist, designing websites, or engaging with new acquaintances, Phil’s commitment to excellence remains unwavering. His journey as a performance guitarist commenced at the age of 13 during his secondary schooling. After auditioning and being accepted into a music school, he delved into classical theory and history.

    Upon completing his classical studies, Phil transitioned to performance jazz, a move that greatly facilitated his understanding and mastery of various music genres. Amidst his jazz training, an opportunity arose for him to embark on an international touring venture. During this intensive period of rehearsals and performances, Phil’s musical growth exceeded that of his previous four years in music schools. Through music, Phil has traversed the globe, encountered countless talented individuals, and embraced diverse cultural experiences.
